Generative instruments.

Small web tools built for design work. Each one takes a process that is hard to draw by hand, gives it a seed and a panel, and exports the result as an image, a mesh or a point cloud.

01 Growth instrument Grow coral, mould and erosion patterns from a typed seed, then export the result as an image, a 3D mesh or a point cloud. Five growth systems, eight colour ramps. Open the instrument → tool ●
Built in the browser. No dependencies, no tracking, nothing loaded from a third party.
